Privacy Policy

MergeMate · Last updated: November 8, 2026

MergeMate (merge-mate-rose.vercel.app) is a free set of PDF and document tools for students. This page explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address when you sign up, and a securely hashed password if you use email and password.
  • Google Sign-In: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account. We do not receive your Google password and we do not access your Google Drive, Gmail or contacts.
  • Session cookie: after you log in we store a secure cookie so that you stay logged in.
  • Files you process: some tools (for example PDF to Word/Excel/PowerPoint, Word/Excel/PowerPoint to PDF and the handwriting generator) send your file or text to our servers to be converted. Files are used only to produce your result and are not kept after processing.
  • Handwriting samples: if you use the handwriting feature, the letter images cut from your photo are saved to your account so you do not have to repeat the setup.
  • Feedback: the message, type and optional email you submit through the feedback form.
  • Usage analytics: we use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate, which pages and tools are used.

2. How we use it

To run your account, sign you in, provide the tools you ask for, answer feedback, and improve the website. We do not sell your personal information and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who we share it with

We use trusted service providers to run the site: Vercel (website hosting), Render (servers), MongoDB Atlas (database), Google (sign-in and analytics). They process data only to provide their service to us.

4. Your choices

You can log out at any time. To have your account and saved handwriting data deleted, or to ask any question about this policy, contact us using the details below.

5. Security

Passwords are stored hashed, connections use HTTPS, and sessions use secure cookies. No online service can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

6. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
